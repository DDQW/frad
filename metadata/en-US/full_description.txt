FRAD lets you strike up a random, anonymous chat with someone physically
near you — no account, no phone number, no company-run server matching you up.

How it works:

* Nearby (Bluetooth LE, no internet needed): your phone and theirs discover
  each other directly over Bluetooth. Matching who you chat with happens on
  your own device. Photos and files go over a direct Wi-Fi connection.
* Wider range: find people within a chosen radius over the internet, using an
  open, decentralized discovery protocol (go-libp2p) — not a company-owned
  matchmaking service. Anyone can run a bootstrap/relay node; server lists can
  be shared between phones by link or QR code.

Privacy and safety by design:

* No account, phone number, or email — ever.
* Your exact GPS coordinates are never sent anywhere, not even to a matched
  peer. Photos and videos you send are stripped of their location and other
  hidden data first.
* Being visible is always shown: while FRAD can be found in the background,
  a permanent notification says so and turns it off with one tap. You can
  also limit visibility to 30 minutes, 1 hour or 3 hours.
* Chats are end-to-end encrypted between the two phones (Noise protocol);
  message lengths are padded so they don't give away what you write.
* Nobody can send you a file you haven't accepted; pictures from people who
  aren't your contacts stay pixelated until you tap them.
* Chats are only kept with people you save as contacts — encrypted on your
  phone, and deleted after a period you choose if you like.
* Optional app lock (fingerprint, face or screen lock) and a "delete
  everything" button.
* Adults and minors are never matched with each other.
* You can block or report anyone you've chatted with — even after they left —
  entirely on-device.

100% free and open source software. No ads, no tracking, no proprietary
network services.
