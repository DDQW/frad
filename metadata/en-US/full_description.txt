FRAD lets you strike up a random, anonymous chat with someone physically
near you — no account, no phone number, no company-run server matching you up.

How it works:

* Nearby (Bluetooth LE, no internet needed): your phone and theirs discover
  each other directly over Bluetooth. Matching who you chat with happens on
  your own device.
* Wider range (planned): find people within a chosen radius over the internet,
  using an open, decentralized discovery protocol — not a company-owned
  matchmaking service.

Privacy and safety by design:

* No account, phone number, or email — ever.
* Your exact GPS coordinates are never sent anywhere, not even to a matched
  peer.
* "Available to chat" is an opt-in toggle you turn on, not something running
  silently in the background.
* Chats are end-to-end encrypted between the two phones.
* You can block or report anyone you're matched with, entirely on-device.

100% free and open source software. No ads, no tracking, no proprietary
network services.
